# Mobile commerce and the La Tour reception

Scope: homepage La Tour editorial scene, product templates, quote request and shared controls. Preserve the approved hero composition and drop, Parfumaire palette/type, catalog data, all variants/prices, routes, and the no-send preview mode. The user requested these changes explicitly, including shared controls; no additional approval was needed.

Baseline: `before-product-390.webp`, `before-quote-390.webp`, corresponding desktop captures. A 390px touch viewport at 4× CPU throttling produced three scrolling long tasks of 155, 100 and 54 ms while related 3D cards initialized.

## Mobbin direction

Use explicit selections and a contained form with a readable summary. The opposite direction, a sparse supplier-message drawer (Programa), does not supply enough product/contact context for this public site. Borrow structure only, never the reference apps' identity or data.

1. **Visible finish/size choices.** Apple HomePod mini uses large bordered colour tiles with clear labels: https://mobbin.com/screens/83a6d574-bd21-463e-9379-d1e2a43f7f1a . The rendered screen was inspected. Applied to the selection panel; retain Parfumaire colours, type, catalog finish/size wording. Image: https://mobbin.com/api/mcp/short/HxYymI2v
2. **Contained product actions.** Lululemon places fulfillment choices and its primary purchase button together beside the gallery: https://mobbin.com/screens/013f6a05-d050-4f9d-a225-2cebc8560981 . Inspected image, applied grouping to finish, quantity, quote action. No shipping claims or checkout added. Image: https://mobbin.com/api/mcp/short/fnKKkQmj
3. **Carry the selection to the quote.** Deel's quote flow presents the selected service/quantity as a readable summary and a distinct completion state: https://mobbin.com/flows/b90943d0-ccbf-44d7-99f6-1a430ca4e7a7 . Screens 1, 3 and 5 inspected. Applied product summary and completion; new Parfumaire copy, no internal IDs shown to visitors. Cross-domain reference supplies structure only. Image of completion: https://mobbin.com/api/mcp/short/vVvl1mDH

Not reference-derived: the native mobile scroll, deferred GPU loading, swipe gesture, mobile quote bar, review step wording and exact rounded styling are implementation decisions for the user's phone-first request. The homepage motion and product dots follow the supplied Bella reference and the user's explicit instructions.

Dropped: Snowflake marketplace, Zara wishlist and GetYourGuide detail were unrelated/weak matches. Faire's wholesale quantity selector and Expedia's hotel-group form add purchase logic that Parfumaire does not have. No claims about conversion or real-device performance are made from these examples.
